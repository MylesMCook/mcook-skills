# Inkblot Mascot

Load this when the character might drift into mascot, sticker, cartoon, or decoration territory.

## Role

Inkblot is the recurring visual character for this US English article-illustration style. Inkblot is not a logo, not a mascot sticker, and not a cute sidekick. It is an earnest backstage operator doing some absurd physical labor that explains the idea.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black abstract blob, not a human.
- White dot eyes; blank, calm, slightly confused but serious expression.
- Thin legs; occasional thin arms when needed for action.
- Slightly uneven hand-drawn outline.
- Body may become a black box, funnel, hole, shadow, bean, gear-room lump, stamp pad, or machine part.
- Keep it simple. No hair, clothes, detailed mouth, eyelashes, shoes, accessories, ethnicity, gender, age, or realistic anatomy.

## Personality

- Serious about a weird job.
- Dry humor, not slapstick.
- Slightly awkward, never stupid.
- Looks like it genuinely belongs inside the system sketch.
- Deadpan, not cute.

## Make Inkblot do the core action

Useful actions: haul, pull, sort, stamp, patch, stitch, weigh, route, block, catch, drop, push, crank, label, cut, plug, fold, filter, bridge, guard, recycle, untangle.

Good examples of role logic:

- If the concept is a handoff, Inkblot physically passes a tiny box across a gap.
- If the concept is trust-building, Inkblot lays receipts like stepping stones.
- If the concept is workflow drag, Inkblot pulls a heavy orange arrow across the page.
- If the concept is information overload, Inkblot sorts a messy pile into three quiet trays.

## Fail conditions

Regenerate or rewrite the prompt if:

- Removing Inkblot leaves the metaphor fully intact.
- Inkblot is just pointing at a diagram.
- Inkblot looks like a brand mascot, toy, sticker, emoji, child character, or animal.
- Inkblot becomes too round, shiny, polished, expressive, or adorable.
- Inkblot’s look could be read as a caricature of a human group.
- Inkblot steals attention from the structure instead of making the structure work.
