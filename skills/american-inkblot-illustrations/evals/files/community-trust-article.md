# Why Community Trust Compounds Slowly

Trust does not arrive because a brand announces values. It shows up when people can point to small, repeated proofs.

A helpful answer, a fair moderation decision, a quick apology, and a promise kept all work like receipts. None of them is impressive alone. Together they become a trail.

The dangerous move is to ask for trust before there is evidence. People can feel that gap immediately.

A community system should make proof visible, make repair easy, and make promises small enough to keep.
