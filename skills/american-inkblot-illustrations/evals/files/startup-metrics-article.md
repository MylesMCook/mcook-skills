# Startup Metrics That Actually Help

Most teams track the easiest numbers first: page views, signups, and likes. Those numbers feel good in a dashboard, but they rarely change what the team does next.

A useful metric creates a decision. It tells you whether to keep pushing, change the offer, fix onboarding, or stop spending time on a channel.

The hard part is that useful metrics are usually less shiny. They show the gap between what a customer tried to do and where they got stuck.

The best teams turn metrics into a weekly operating loop: pick one friction point, talk to users around it, ship one fix, and check whether the number moves.
