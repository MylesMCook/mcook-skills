# Notice

This skill is a US English localization and redesign inspired by the MIT-licensed
`helloianneo/ian-xiaohei-illustrations` project.

The original project targets Chinese article illustrations with the Xiaohei visual
character, clean white 16:9 hand-drawn style, and sparse red/orange/blue Chinese
annotations. This derivative changes the runtime job to US English article body
illustrations, renames the character to Inkblot for US-facing clarity, removes
Chinese annotation defaults, and adds localization guardrails against patriotic
clichés or racialized readings.

No original example images are bundled.
