# Law of Pragnanz

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/law-of-pr%C3%A4gnanz/

## When to load

Load this reference when icons, diagrams, visualizations, complex layouts, ambiguous patterns, decorative shapes, or hard-to-parse imagery.

## Lens

People interpret complex or ambiguous visual information in the simplest form available.

## Look for in the design

- visual elements whose simplest interpretation is not the intended one
- icons or graphics with unnecessary detail
- layouts where the user must decode shape before meaning

## Critique moves

- Simplify visual forms until the intended meaning is the easiest interpretation.
- Remove decorative complexity that competes with recognition.
- Clarify ambiguous graphics with labels, alignment, or simpler structure.

## Watch-out

Do not simplify data or controls so far that users lose necessary distinctions.

## Pairs well with

Occam's Razor, Cognitive Load, Law of Similarity
