# Selective Attention

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/selective-attention/

## When to load

Load this reference when busy pages, banners, notifications, alerts, dashboards, ads, modals, change states, calls to action, or visual competition.

## Lens

People focus on a subset of stimuli, usually those connected to their goal, and filter out the rest.

## Look for in the design

- important content styled like ads or low-priority banners
- multiple simultaneous attention-grabbers
- state changes with weak cues that users may miss

## Critique moves

- Align visual emphasis with the user's active goal and remove competing signals.
- Make critical changes noticeable with context, placement, and timing.
- Avoid ad-like styling for content users must actually read.

## Watch-out

Do not make everything prominent; selective attention works only when priority is scarce.

## Pairs well with

Von Restorff Effect, Cognitive Load, Law of Similarity
