# Law of Uniform Connectedness

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/law-of-uniform-connectedness/

## When to load

Load this reference when steppers, timelines, flow diagrams, tables, grouped controls, nested items, relationships, or status connections.

## Lens

Visually connected elements are perceived as more related than unconnected elements.

## Look for in the design

- connected items with no visible link
- lines/arrows suggesting relationships that do not exist
- multi-step or parent-child structures that rely on text alone

## Critique moves

- Use lines, arrows, shared color, or connected containers to show real relationships.
- Remove connectors that imply false dependency.
- Clarify sequence, ownership, or grouping visually when users must act on relationships.

## Watch-out

Do not add connection marks as decoration; every connector should answer a relationship question.

## Pairs well with

Law of Common Region, Law of Proximity, Goal-Gradient Effect
