# Parkinson's Law

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/parkinsons-law/

## When to load

Load this reference when forms, checkout, booking, setup, profile completion, task timing, estimates, autosave/autofill, or lengthy workflows.

## Lens

Tasks tend to expand to fill the time available or expected.

## Look for in the design

- small tasks stretched across unnecessary steps
- manual entry where autofill/defaults could reduce duration
- flows whose perceived length exceeds user expectations

## Critique moves

- Compress routine tasks to match or beat user expectations.
- Use autofill, smart defaults, saved information, and bulk actions to prevent task inflation.
- Make time cost visible when a task is legitimately long.

## Watch-out

Do not rush high-stakes decisions where users need time to verify details.

## Pairs well with

Doherty Threshold, Pareto Principle, Goal-Gradient Effect
