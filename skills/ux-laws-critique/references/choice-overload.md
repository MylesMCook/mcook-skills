# Choice Overload

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/choice-overload/

## When to load

Load this reference when menus, product lists, pricing, filters, settings, cards, templates, recommendations, or any moment with many alternatives.

## Lens

Too many comparable options can overwhelm users and degrade decision quality.

## Look for in the design

- large option sets presented before users know how to choose
- similar-looking choices without comparison criteria
- filters/search/recommendations hidden after the overload has already happened

## Critique moves

- Reduce the first decision surface to the few options that match the user's likely goal.
- Add narrowing tools before the option set: search, filters, sorting, guided questions, or recommended defaults.
- Enable side-by-side comparison when choosing is the task.

## Watch-out

Do not simply delete options if the user still needs them; stage or group them so choice happens at the right time.

## Pairs well with

Hick's Law, Cognitive Load, Miller's Law
