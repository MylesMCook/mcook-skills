# Goal-Gradient Effect

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/goal-gradient-effect/

## When to load

Load this reference when multi-step forms, onboarding, checkout, setup, progress bars, profile completion, achievements, or migration flows.

## Lens

Motivation tends to rise as people can see they are closer to completing a goal.

## Look for in the design

- long tasks with no visible progress
- progress indicators that fail to show proximity to completion
- completion moments that feel ambiguous or unrewarding

## Critique moves

- Show progress in terms users care about: steps left, completion percentage, or next milestone.
- Make the final stretch feel attainable by reducing friction near the end.
- Use honest head starts or saved defaults when they reflect real progress.

## Watch-out

Do not fake progress in ways that damage trust; artificial progress must be transparent or harmless.

## Pairs well with

Zeigarnik Effect, Doherty Threshold, Peak-End Rule
