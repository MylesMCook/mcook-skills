# Peak-End Rule

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/peak-end-rule/

## When to load

Load this reference when onboarding, checkout completion, errors, cancellation, waiting, confirmation, support, success states, or journey reviews.

## Lens

People remember an experience strongly through its most intense moments and its ending, not by averaging every moment.

## Look for in the design

- emotional peaks such as errors, risk, waiting, payment, or account creation
- flat or confusing endings after important effort
- negative peaks that are avoidable or recoverable

## Critique moves

- Improve the most emotionally intense moment before polishing low-impact details.
- Design clear, reassuring endings with confirmation, next steps, and recovery paths.
- Reduce or reframe negative peaks such as uncertainty, hidden costs, or unexplained waits.

## Watch-out

Do not add delight that distracts from unresolved stress, risk, or failure.

## Pairs well with

Aesthetic-Usability Effect, Doherty Threshold, Goal-Gradient Effect
