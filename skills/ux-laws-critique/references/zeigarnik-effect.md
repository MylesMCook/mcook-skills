# Zeigarnik Effect

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/zeigarnik-effect/

## When to load

Load this reference when progressive disclosure, saved drafts, onboarding, profile completion, task resumption, partial setup, tabs, or content discovery.

## Lens

People tend to remember incomplete or interrupted tasks more than completed ones.

## Look for in the design

- unfinished tasks with no visible reminder or resumption path
- additional content hidden without signifiers
- progress that disappears after interruption

## Critique moves

- Make incomplete tasks visible and easy to resume.
- Use clear signifiers for additional content or next steps.
- Show progress or saved state so interruption creates motivation rather than anxiety.

## Watch-out

Do not exploit incompletion to create nagging or manipulative loops; support user goals.

## Pairs well with

Goal-Gradient Effect, Working Memory, Peak-End Rule
