# Flow

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/flow/

## When to load

Load this reference when creative tools, productivity flows, onboarding, learning, search/refinement, configuration, dashboards, games, or repetitive work.

## Lens

People stay engaged when challenge, skill, control, feedback, and momentum are in balance.

## Look for in the design

- interruptions that break task momentum
- missing feedback about progress or completed action
- tasks that are too hard for novices or too slow for experienced users

## Critique moves

- Remove unnecessary mode switches and dead ends in the core task path.
- Provide frequent, meaningful feedback so users know what happened and what remains.
- Offer progressive capability: simple entry for novices, shortcuts or bulk actions for experts.

## Watch-out

Do not over-optimize for uninterrupted flow when users need reflection, consent, or risk checks.

## Pairs well with

Doherty Threshold, Paradox of the Active User, Goal-Gradient Effect
