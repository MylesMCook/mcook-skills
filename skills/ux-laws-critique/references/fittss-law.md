# Fitts's Law

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/fittss-law/

## When to load

Load this reference when buttons, touch targets, menus, destructive actions, toolbars, floating actions, form controls, gestures, mobile layouts, or high-frequency actions.

## Lens

The effort to hit a target depends on target size and distance from the user's current focus or input position.

## Look for in the design

- small or crowded interactive controls
- primary actions far from where the user just worked
- high-risk targets placed too close to safe/common targets

## Critique moves

- Increase target size and spacing for frequent, important, or high-risk actions.
- Move primary actions closer to the task context and likely pointer/thumb path.
- Separate destructive or irreversible controls from routine controls.

## Watch-out

Do not enlarge every control equally; prioritize frequency, risk, and task sequence.

## Pairs well with

Law of Proximity, Doherty Threshold, Hick's Law
