# Cognitive Bias

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/cognitive-bias/

## When to load

Load this reference when reviewing persuasive UI, recommendations, defaults, risk messaging, comparisons, confirmations, ranking, or reviewer assumptions.

## Lens

Human judgment uses shortcuts that help people decide quickly but can also distort interpretation and behavior.

## Look for in the design

- defaults that steer decisions without explanation
- copy or layout that overconfirms one interpretation
- design-review claims based on designer assumptions rather than user evidence

## Critique moves

- Name the likely bias and the exact UI element that may trigger it.
- Recommend clearer evidence, balanced comparison, or reversible choices where bias could harm users.
- Separate helpful decision support from manipulation.

## Watch-out

Do not invoke cognitive bias generically; name the behavior and impact in this specific interface.

## Pairs well with

Peak-End Rule, Serial Position Effect, Von Restorff Effect
