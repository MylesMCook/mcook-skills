# Pareto Principle

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/pareto-principle/

## When to load

Load this reference when prioritizing fixes, feature sets, dashboards, user segments, analytics, common tasks, or high-impact design debt.

## Lens

In many systems, a small number of causes, tasks, or user segments create a large share of outcomes.

## Look for in the design

- equal visual weight for rare and common actions
- design effort spent on edge cases before the dominant path works
- dashboards or menus that do not prioritize high-value tasks

## Critique moves

- Prioritize the critical 20% of tasks, screens, or controls that likely drive most value.
- Make frequent/high-impact paths easier and faster than rare paths.
- Ask for data when priority claims are speculative.

## Watch-out

Do not use 80/20 as a precise statistic without evidence; use it as a prioritization lens.

## Pairs well with

Occam's Razor, Parkinson's Law, Hick's Law
