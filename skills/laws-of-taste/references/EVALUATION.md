# Evaluation

Use this scorecard for audits, QA, or final polish passes.

## Rate each area

Use:
- Pass
- Needs work
- Fails the brief

Or, when more nuance is useful:
- Strong
- Adequate
- Weak

## Areas

### 1. Hierarchy
- Is there a clear first read?
- Are secondary and tertiary levels genuinely secondary?
- Can a skimming user still understand what matters?

### 2. Structure
- Is there an ordering system?
- Do alignment, grouping, and spacing feel intentional?
- If the grid is broken, is the break meaningful?

### 3. Typography
- Does the type suit the brand and content?
- Is body text comfortable?
- Are headings convincing rather than merely large?
- Do small text and metadata still look designed?

### 4. Color
- Does color create meaning, grouping, and emphasis?
- Is the palette working in context, not only in isolation?
- Are accents scarce enough to matter?

### 5. Shape / Components
- Do components feel like one family?
- Are cards, inputs, buttons, tables, and navigation equally considered?
- Is unnecessary chrome removed?

### 6. Imagery
- Does imagery contribute mood, proof, structure, or narrative?
- Is cropping assertive enough?
- Is the amount of imagery justified?

### 7. Interaction / Motion
- Do transitions clarify change?
- Are hover/focus/active/error states consistent?
- Is behavior calmer and clearer than the styling suggests?

### 8. Accessibility floor
- Is text legible at realistic sizes and distances for the medium?
- Is contrast strong enough for the actual environment, not just the mockup?
- Are states and categories visible without relying on color alone?
- Are motion and timing restrained enough to avoid slowing the task?

### 9. Responsiveness
- Does the concept survive on the smallest screen?
- Is mobile recomposed or merely collapsed?
- Do spacing, hierarchy, and density adapt intelligently?

### 10. Finish
- Are dividers, labels, captions, chart notes, icons, and edge conditions polished?
- Is repetition coherent without becoming monotonous?

## Final verdict prompts

- What is the single strongest thing about the design?
- What is the single most generic thing left in it?
- What should be removed next?
- What one change would create the biggest jump in perceived taste?
- What accessibility risk is still unresolved?
