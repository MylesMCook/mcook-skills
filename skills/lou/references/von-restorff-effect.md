# Von Restorff Effect

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/von-restorff-effect/

## When to load

Load this reference when primary CTA, pricing tiers, alerts, featured items, comparison tables, status messages, onboarding prompts, or visual emphasis.

## Lens

Among similar items, the one that stands out is more likely to be noticed and remembered.

## Look for in the design

- too many elements competing for distinctive treatment
- important actions that do not stand out from peers
- standout styling that makes content look like an ad or warning

## Critique moves

- Make the one truly important item distinctive through size, contrast, placement, or shape.
- Reduce competing emphasis so salience has meaning.
- Pair color with labels/icons/shape so the distinction does not rely on color alone.

## Watch-out

Do not highlight multiple competing actions; if everything is special, nothing is.

## Pairs well with

Selective Attention, Law of Similarity, Fitts's Law
