# Law of Common Region

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/law-of-common-region/

## When to load

Load this reference when cards, panels, forms, settings groups, dashboard widgets, comparisons, empty states, or any grouping problem.

## Lens

Elements inside a shared boundary or background are perceived as belonging together.

## Look for in the design

- related items lacking a visible container
- containers that group unrelated elements
- boundaries that make the wrong relationship look intentional

## Critique moves

- Use cards, panels, background areas, or borders to clarify which controls and information belong together.
- Remove containers that falsely group unrelated content.
- Match grouping strength to task importance: stronger boundaries for high-stakes or dense areas.

## Watch-out

Do not add boxes everywhere; excessive boundaries add noise and reduce scan speed.

## Pairs well with

Law of Proximity, Law of Uniform Connectedness, Chunking
