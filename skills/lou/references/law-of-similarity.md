# Law of Similarity

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/law-of-similarity/

## When to load

Load this reference when buttons, links, cards, typography, icons, status badges, navigation, repeated modules, or design-system consistency.

## Lens

Elements that look alike are perceived as related or equivalent, even when separated.

## Look for in the design

- different actions styled identically
- same-type content styled inconsistently
- links or interactive elements that look like plain text

## Critique moves

- Make equivalent elements share visual treatment and different elements diverge intentionally.
- Differentiate links, navigation, primary actions, and destructive actions by more than copy alone.
- Use similarity to help users predict behavior across the interface.

## Watch-out

Do not force perfect visual uniformity if status, hierarchy, or risk needs distinction.

## Pairs well with

Von Restorff Effect, Law of Proximity, Jakob's Law
