# Law of Proximity

Source concept: https://lawsofux.com/law-of-proximity/

## When to load

Load this reference when spacing, labels and fields, card metadata, buttons, lists, tables, navigation, or visual hierarchy concerns.

## Lens

Objects placed near each other are perceived as related.

## Look for in the design

- labels closer to the wrong fields
- actions visually detached from the content they affect
- unrelated items so close they appear grouped

## Critique moves

- Tighten spacing inside a group and increase spacing between groups.
- Place controls directly next to the content or state they affect.
- Use spacing as the first grouping tool before adding lines or boxes.

## Watch-out

Do not treat whitespace only as aesthetics; critique whether it creates correct relationships.

Law of Common Region, Fitts's Law, Law of Similarity
