## Risk categories

- Architecture: wrong boundary, coupling, leaky abstraction, scalability constraint.
- Data: corruption, loss, privacy, migration failure, consistency gap.
- Security: auth/authz, secrets, abuse, supply chain, compliance.
- Delivery: estimate, dependency, scope, staffing, late integration.
- Operations: observability, rollback, incident response, capacity.
- Team: ownership, bus factor, coordination, skill gap.
